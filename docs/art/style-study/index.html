Recommended

A · Storybook toon

Three-step cel shading, dark outlines, bright and slightly saturated colours. Silhouettes stay readable when a character is a thumb-width tall.

Phone cost
Low shading cost. Outlines double draw calls for each character, so draw them only within about 10 cells.
Art work
Flat colour textures in one atlas per rig, which fits ADR-002 row 179. Skin and hair colour stay a colour parameter.
Risk
Needs a disciplined palette, or it looks like a children's game.
Cheapest

B · Faceted low-poly

Flat-shaded facets with no textures, only vertex colours. Clean and geometric.

Phone cost
The lowest: few triangles, no textures, one surface per character.
Art work
The fastest to make, and closest to today's generated placeholders.
Risk
Faces carry little expression, and the look is common in asset stores.
Most atmospheric

C · Soft painted

Smooth shading, warm sky light, soft shadows and haze, aiming for hand-painted textures.

Phone cost
The highest: PBR materials, soft shadows and fog on the Mobile renderer. It needs on-device measuring first (T-803).
Art work
The most: a hand-painted texture atlas for each rig and each piece of gear.
Risk
Small characters get muddy at game distance without strong rim light.

These are quick mock-ups built from simple shapes in the browser, not final art. They show shading, palette, proportions and readability, which are what an art direction decides. Every character and design is original to this project.
