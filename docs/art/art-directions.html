Four ways Starter Meadow could look

The same corner of the meadow, the same fixed 3/4 camera, rendered live in four art styles: a Novice with a sword, two Gel Slimes, the guide, a pond, trees, rocks and a fence line, plus the tap-to-move marker.

iThese are style samples, not game screenshots. They run in your browser with simple shapes so only the lighting, shading and palette differ between options. Final models will be sculpted in Blender per the style you pick, and the theme system means the choice can be changed later without touching code or content.
A · toon

Cel-shaded toon

My pick

Flat bands of light and shadow with ink outlines. Bright, cheerful and very easy to read on a small screen.

Phone readability
GPU cost
Art effort

Works well

  • Silhouettes pop at 3/4 distance
  • Ages well; no chase for realism
  • Cheap shader, mobile friendly

Watch out

  • Outlines add draw calls; keep them to characters
  • Needs a consistent colour script
B · low-poly

Faceted low-poly

Cheapest art

Hard-edged facets and pastel colours, no textures. Many free CC0 asset packs already look like this.

Phone readability
GPU cost
Art effort

Works well

  • Fastest to fill a whole world
  • Lightest on battery and GPU
  • Easy for one person to keep consistent

Watch out

  • Can look generic without a strong palette
  • Characters read as simple; less gear detail
C · soft painted

Soft stylized

Most premium

Smooth shading, warm sunlight, soft shadows and haze. Feels rich and storybook-like, closer to a console game.

Phone readability
GPU cost
Art effort

Works well

  • Best first impression in store screenshots
  • Gear and materials can show detail

Watch out

  • Hand-painted textures for every asset
  • Soft shadows and fog strain the 30 fps budget on mid-range phones
D · pixel 3D

Retro pixel 3D

Most distinctive

The 3D world is rendered at low resolution and scaled up with crisp pixels; the UI stays sharp. Nostalgic and very cheap.

When you decide

  1. The look-dev spike builds the chosen style in Godot: one character, the Gel Slime and a corner of Starter Meadow, measured on the phone against the 30 fps budget.
  2. It writes a one-page style guide (palette, proportions, triangle and texture limits) that every later asset follows.
  3. The art lands in themes/default. A second theme can carry a different style later; game content never changes.
