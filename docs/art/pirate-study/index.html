Saltwind Crews

An original high-seas setting: scattered islands, rival crews, and bounties. Four jobs and two maps, drawn in the storybook toon style you chose. They're seen through the game's own camera (50° pitch, 30° field of view). Switch on "Game distance" to see them at the default zoom.

Tide relics

Powers come from tide relics: coral, shell and sea-glass trinkets dredged from wrecks. A relic is worn in an accessory slot and grants one skill. You can swap relics, and a crew can trade them, so power is something you find and carry, not something you're born with.

That uses systems the game already has: equipment slots, cards, and skills granted by items.

What would change in the project

  • A new theme folder (themes/saltwind/) with models, palette and sounds. No code changes for the look.
  • New content: jobs, skills, monsters (Reef Crab, Bilge Rat, Drowned Sailor), items and the two maps.
  • One vocabulary addition: a musket weapon type, plus a club if wanted.
  • Everything original. No names, symbols or designs from existing pirate stories.

These are quick mock-ups built from simple shapes in the browser, not final art. They show the setting, silhouettes, palette and how readable the crew is at game distance.
