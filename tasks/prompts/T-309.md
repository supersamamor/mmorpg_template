### Goal
T-307's live smoke test found a problem. A brand-new character (all stats 1, its 10 starting points unspent, no weapon) deals 1 damage to
a Gel Slime and takes 6 per hit, so it dies about 10 times before the slime falls. The first fight a player has must be winnable without
reading a guide.

### Targets (the decision; tune the data until these hold)
| Matchup | Must hold |
|---|---|
| New character, **0 points spent**, no weapon vs `monster.gel_slime` (passive) | Wins one-on-one in ≤ 12 swings on average, ending with ≥ 30% HP |
| Same, with the 10 points spent by a simple rule (all into `str`, or split `str`/`vit`) | Wins in ≤ 5 swings, ≥ 60% HP left |
| Level 3, points spent, no weapon vs `monster.mossling` | Wins with ≥ 20% HP left |
| Level 5–6, points spent, no weapon vs the meadow's toughest monster | Roughly an even fight (40–70% expected win rate). Danger stays real. |

"On average" means the expected value from the `stats` model, using hit chance, crit chance and both sides' `aspd`. Also confirm the
first matchup with a seeded simulation of 1,000 fights through the real `CombatResolver`.
