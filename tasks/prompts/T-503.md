### Goal
Players can spend stat points, and can undo their choices with a reset item.
- `ProgressionRules.RaiseStat` already exists, but nothing on the wire or in the client uses it, so stat points pile up unspent. Add the intent and a stats panel.
- Add **respec as data**: consumable items whose effects reset stats or skills and refund the points, with a data-driven cost. NPC services (T-601) can reuse the same effects later.

T-222 (toon rendering) runs in parallel on the client's look. Stay out of materials, lighting, `GearMesher`, `ModelStore` and `themegen`, and keep your skill-panel changes small.

### Requirements
1. **Raise stats.** `raise_stat` (stat id, amount) runs `ProgressionRules.RaiseStat` on the server, using the cost from `balance.yaml` `stat_point_cost` and the stat range. Every refusal has a reason term. The derived stats update the same tick, through the existing `base` modifier source. The own-progress message carries base stats, unspent stat points and each stat's next cost, so the client never computes a cost.
2. **Reset effects.** Two new effect types:
   - `reset_stats`: every allocated stat back to the start value, refunding exactly the points spent;
   - `reset_skills`: every learned skill back to 0, refunding exactly the skill points spent, with passives removed, running casts cancelled and cooldowns kept.

   They run through T-501's runner, so an item, and later an NPC service, uses them as data. Add both to VOCABULARY.md. They're load-validated: only on a consumable or service, and only targeting `self`.
3. **Reset rules as data.** In `balance.yaml` (not formulas), decide and document the rules, for example:
   - a free reset below a base level;
   - whether a reset is refused while in combat (regeneration's out-of-combat delay already exists) or while dead.

   Every refusal has a reason term, and nothing is used up on a refusal.
4. **Items.** Add two original-named reset items (one for stats, one for skills) with weights and descriptions. Give new characters one of each in `new_character.start_items`, or add a drop source; choose one and say why.
5. **Client.**
   - A stats panel: the six stats, unspent points, a + button with the next cost from the server, and the derived stats that matter (ATK, MATK, DEF, HIT, FLEE, ASPD, max HP and SP).
   - The skill tree panel refreshes after a reset.
   - Using a reset item asks for confirmation in the bag.
   - The client's words go in `UiText`; numbers come from the server. Keep T-211's touch rules and the §9 budgets.
6. **Tests.**
   - Raising stats and every refusal;
   - the exact refund for both resets after mixed spending;
   - passives off after a reset;
   - the free-reset level and the combat rule;
   - load validation of the new effect types;
   - the wire fields;
   - golden values: a level 10 character's derived stats before and after a stat reset;
   - a scripted client run step: spend stat points, use the stat reset, see the points come back.
