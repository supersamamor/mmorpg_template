### Goal
Quests work end to end, driven by data and the event bus (§6.4, hard rule 7). Quests never call combat, the inventory or dialogue directly.
- A player accepts a quest through a dialogue choice (T-601's `start_quest` raises `QuestStartRequested`).
- Objectives advance from events.
- Turning in through dialogue completes a step.
- Rewards are paid atomically.

The four existing quests (First Hunt, Gear Up, the Warden's Trial and the Warden's Oath) become playable, so a Wanderer can become a Warden (rows 211, 212).

### Requirements
1. **State machine.**
   - Per character, per quest: not started, active (step index and per-objective counters), completed. Kept in the progression store with `CompletedQuests`.
   - **Accepting.** `QuestStartRequested` checks the prerequisites (base level, job ids, completed quest ids) and that the quest isn't active or (unless `repeatable`) completed. Every refusal has a reason term that goes to the player.
   - **Abandoning.** `abandon_quest` drops progress and gives nothing back.
   - **Limit.** A data-driven `quests.max_active` goes in `balance.yaml`.
2. **Objectives from events.** Each objective type in VOCABULARY listens to the bus and only counts while its step is current:
   - `kill`: the kill credit rule from §6.7. Decide and document it: the top damager, or everyone with a damage share.
   - `collect`: counts the bag's quantity. The step completes when every objective is met. `consume: true` takes the items at turn-in, atomically (hard rule 10).
   - `talk`: an `NpcTalked` with that NPC while the step is current.
   - `reach`: a cell and radius on a map.
   - `level`: base level at least N, already met when the step starts.
   - `job`: the current job.
   - `use_item`: `ItemUsed`.
3. **Turning in and rewards.**
   - A step with only a `talk` objective completes when the player talks to that NPC. The dialogue must show the right node, so add quest **conditions** to T-601's seam: quest state is / step is. Also add the "out of combat" condition from row 253 and use it on the free resets.
   - **Rewards** are paid in one transaction: `exp` (through `ProgressionRules`), `item` (all or nothing when the bag is full: decide and document whether the quest waits or the items drop to the ground), `currency`, `skill_point`, `stat_point`, `unlock_job` (`UnlockedJobs`, so T-502's job change accepts it), and `status`.
   - **Currency** doesn't exist yet. Add a minimal per-character balance, in memory, shown in the bag. Its VOCABULARY term already exists.
4. **Data.**

   - Make the Warden path work: Warden's Trial, then unlock, then `change_job`, then Warden's Oath.
   - Every quest is validated at load: objective ids exist, the reachability of the giver and turn-in NPC, and the reward ids.
5. **Client.**

   - A compact tracker under the HUD for one quest.
   - A marker over NPCs with a quest to offer or turn in, sent by the server as a per-player flag on the NPC's view record or in the quest messages (decide).
   - The client never decides progress. The client's own words go in `UiText`. Keep T-211, the §9 budgets and the T-222 look.
