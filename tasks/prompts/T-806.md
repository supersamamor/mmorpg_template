### Goal
Staff can moderate and fix things through T-804's admin API, every change going through the game, checked by role and audited: the commands of ARCHITECTURE §12.4 "Moderation", "Support fixes" and "Server", and the support queue of "Store compliance".

### Requirements
1. **Ban** (`gm`+): timed or permanent, account level, with a reason. Refused at sign-in and at `hello` with its own answer (until when, for a timed one; never the staff reason unless you decide it is shown, and say why), and a live game ends at once. Unban. A ban survives restarts and holds on every server process (the database decides; row 320's limit applies to the live session only: say how long another process may keep a banned player).
2. **Mute** (`support`+): timed, account level. There is no chat yet (T-901): store it, expose it to the game as a check T-901 will call ("may this account speak now?"), and show it in the reads. Unmute.
3. **Support fixes** (`gm`+): give or remove items (new items get new instance ids and `source = admin`, §12.3, so dupe audits tell them apart; say where `source` lives) and currency on a character, in the world or not, and move a stuck character to its save point. A character in the world is changed on its tick (`AdminRoad`); one that is not is changed in the database with its claim rules (T-702): never both at once, never lost by a save that lands later. Before and after in the audit row.
4. **Server** (`gm`+ announce, `admin` maintenance): a server-wide announcement every client shows; maintenance mode that blocks new logins with its own answer, warns those playing and, after a set time, ends their games (saved).
5. **Support queue** (`support`+): deletion and data-export requests that come in through support. A deletion request from support uses T-703's path (with the waiting time), not a shortcut. A data export produces the account's data as JSON for staff to send (what T-703's docs list as stored), audited. No e-mail is sent by the server.
6. **Reads**: an account's bans, mutes and support requests; a character's item and currency changes made by staff.
7. **Client**: the ban and maintenance answers and the announcement in `UiText`, within the HUD budgets, the new lines in the `ui` matrix with 0 problems.
8. **Erasing** (T-703): bans, mutes, requests and anything else stored about the account go with it, except what docs/STORE_COMPLIANCE.md lets you keep (decide: a ban of an erased account?); the erasure test must see it. Update that document.
9. **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE §12, ADMIN_API.md (every new endpoint), VOCABULARY.
10. **Tests** for every rule with the real database where it matters: role table, each command's refusals, ban at sign-in, `hello` and live, mute check, item give and remove in and out of the world (with a save racing), maintenance, the queue, the export's content, erasing, nothing secret or personal in logs. One e2e run: a scripted client plays, staff gives it an item (it sees it), bans it (kicked and refused at sign-in), unbans it, it signs in.
