### Goal
The client's temporary HUD becomes a real mobile UI shell: safe-area aware, scalable, with touch targets at the required size, a hotbar ready for skills, and full touch input including long-press and pinch. A desktop mouse still works through the same path.

### Requirements
1. **Safe area.** All Control nodes are anchored to the device safe area (notches, home indicator), recalculated on rotation or resize. A debug setting simulates a notch inset on desktop, so this can be tested without a phone.
2. **Touch targets.** Minimum 48 dp on Android and 44 pt on iOS, as pure rules with tests, using the screen's density. World-picking radius stays at least as generous.
3. **UI scale.** A UI-scale setting, stored in user preferences, clamped to a config range. Text is never baked into textures.
4. **Input.** One input path for touch and mouse.
   - Tap, as now.
   - Long-press on an entity shows a small info panel from bundle data only. Long-press must not also fire a tap.
   - Pinch and scroll zoom the camera within its clamp. A pinch must not fire a tap.
   - Taps on UI never reach the world.
5. **Hotbar.** A row of slots. Slot count and layout are client config. Slots are empty placeholders until T-502, and pressing one does nothing visible beyond feedback. Define the seam T-502 will fill. The client sends no skill intents yet.
6. **HUD.** Move HP, SP, EXP, target frame, connection state, and the death, refusal and update screens into the shell. Keep every behaviour T-206 and T-216 tested. No text gets cut off at 16:9 or 19.5:9 at any allowed UI scale.
7. **UI text (row 120).** Decide where the client's own words live, and say why. Leave a clear place for translation later; don't build localisation now. No content ids or numbers in C#.
8. **Budgets (§9).** 30 fps cap in menus, no per-frame work when nothing changes, no per-frame allocation after warm-up. Report draw calls added by the UI.
9. **Tests.** Target-size rule, gesture classification (tap, long-press, pinch, drag thresholds), UI-scale clamp, safe-area layout math, the hotbar seam. Keep gesture logic pure in `client/Core`.
