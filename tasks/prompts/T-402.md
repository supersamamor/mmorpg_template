### Requirements
1. **Cards.** `insert_card` puts a card entry into a free card slot of an equipment entry, if the card's `slots` allow that equipment's slot. It's permanent; removing cards is a later service. The card's modifiers count only while the equipment is worn, with their own source (§6.1). One stack unit is used up. Every refusal has a reason term. It's atomic (hard rule 10).
2. **Refining.** `refine` raises a `refinable` equipment entry's refine level by one, using a material item, with the rules **as data** in a new content file:
   - per level: the success chance, the material item id and quantity, and what a failure does (`keep`, `down` one level or `break` the item);
   - a maximum level.
   Roll with the instance's injected random source. Validate every id at load. The weapon's refine reaches combat's `refine` variable when it's worn. Decide how refine affects armour, using only existing formula variables and modifiers; if it can't be done without a formula change, stop and ask. Add at least one original-named material item, and a drop source for it, so the scripted run can refine.
3. **Inventory entries.** Equipment entries carry their cards and refine level through every move (equip, unequip, drop, pick up), and in the own-inventory messages. Other players see neither.
4. **Client.** The bag panel shows the cards and refine level on an equipment entry. Tapping a card offers the equipment it fits. Tapping a refinable item shows the chance, material and failure outcome from data before you confirm. A result is shown for success, failure and break. The client's own words go in `UiText`; the numbers come from the server or the bundle, never from client constants. Keep T-211's rules and the §9 budgets.
5. **Display bundle (§5.4).** Show the refine data only as far as the player needs to decide: chance, material and outcome. Never send drop rates.
6. **Tests.** Card fits and refusals, modifiers on and off with the equipment, refine success, keep, down and break with a seeded random source, the max level, load validation of the refine file, entries keeping cards and refine through every move, golden values for one refined weapon hit, the own-inventory message, and a scripted run step: socket a card, refine once, and see the stats change.
