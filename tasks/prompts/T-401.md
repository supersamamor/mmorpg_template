### Goal
A player can pick up what monsters drop, carry it within a weight limit, use consumables, and equip and unequip gear that changes their stats and attack. The server decides everything; the client shows the inventory and sends intents.

### Requirements
1. **Inventory model.** Per character, in an `IInventoryStore` shared by all map instances (in memory until T-702). Items as stacks up to `max_stack`; an equipment item is one entry with its own instance id (T-402 will add cards and refine level to it). A slot count limit from `balance.yaml`. Weight is the sum of `weight × quantity`; `weight_limit` comes from `formulas.yaml`. Decide and document what happens at 50% and 90% of the limit (for example regeneration stops, attacking stops), with the numbers in `balance.yaml`; pick-ups that would go over 100% are refused.
2. **Atomic moves (hard rule 10).** Every change (pick up, use, equip, unequip, drop to ground) either applies fully or not at all, inside one tick. Write the operations so T-702 can wrap each in one database transaction without changing the rules. Test that a refused operation changes nothing.
3. **Intents.** `pick_up` (a ground item entity: the player walks into range, then `LootRules.MayPickUp`, then weight and slots), `use_item`, `equip`, `unequip`, `drop_item` (puts a ground item on the player's cell with no owner). Each refusal has a reason term in VOCABULARY.md and goes back as `rejected`. Rate limits follow the existing intent rules.
4. **Use.** Consumables check their `cooldown_ms` (per item id, server time) and `consumed`. Their effects go through one seam that T-501 will own. In this task implement only `heal` and `restore_sp` behind that seam, evaluating amounts as expressions the way formulas are evaluated; a consumable with any other effect type is refused with a reason (nothing used up) until T-501. Say which content items that affects.
5. **Equip.** Check `slots` (any one of the listed slots), `min_base_level`, `job_ids`, and the kind. Equipping into a filled slot swaps atomically. Equipment modifiers go onto the stat sheet with their source (§6.1) and come off cleanly on unequip; the weapon gives `weapon_atk`, `attack_range` and element to combat's `AttackContext` (today they are 0). Two-handed weapons and shields: decide the rule from data (`weapon_type` and slots), and say what you decided.
6. **Wire (§10).** Your own inventory and equipment: a full message on login and resume, then changes only, with a change counter like the other own-state messages.
