### Goal
The client plays sound exactly as §7.8 describes: every sound by asset key through the theme, with the fallback chain, music and ambience per map, crossfades, a voice pool with cooldowns, buses with player volume settings, and proper mobile behaviour. The default theme has only one silent file (T-306 brings real sounds later), so the system must be provable without audible audio.

### Scope
- `client/Core/Audio/**` (pure, tested), `client/Scene/Audio/**`, small wiring in `client/Scene` (`Main`, `WorldPresenter`, `Hud` for the volume settings)
- `client/project.godot` for the bus layout, `tests/Game.Tests/Client/**`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §7.8, `docs/TASKS.md` (this row only)

### Out of scope
- `src/**` (use `AudioResolver` as it is; if it's wrong, stop and say so), `content/**`, real sound files (T-306), combat visuals (T-303 runs in parallel), input (T-219 runs in parallel)

### Requirements
1. **Resolution.** Every play request goes through `AudioResolver` and the theme chain (§7.7). A missing key falls back as §7.8 says, ending in silence with one warning per key, never a crash. Pick a variation at random from the entry's files, using an injected random source; apply `volume_db`, a random pitch from its range, and its bus.
2. **What plays.** Map music and ambience on map load, crossfading on a map change or warp. Monster aggro, hit and die sounds; weapon swing and impact from theme defaults by weapon type, on the same impact timing T-303 uses (agree on one shared timing source, and say which). Item use and equip hooks are ready for T-401. `audio.events` for level up, item acquired and the UI tap.
3. **Voice pool (§9).** At most 24 voices; a per-key cooldown so 30 hits in one tick don't stack; voice stealing by priority then age; sounds far outside the camera are skipped. These numbers are client config. No allocation per sound after warm-up.
4. **Buses and settings.** Buses `music`, `sfx`, `ui` and `ambient`. Player volume settings per bus, stored with the T-211 user preferences and shown in the HUD settings. Mute works.
5. **Mobile.** Pause audio when the app goes to the background and resume when it returns. On iOS, effects follow the silent switch and music is the player's choice. On Android, give up audio focus when another app plays. Where Godot 4.7.2 can't do a part from C#, say exactly what's missing and what it would take, such as a plugin. Don't fake it.
6. **Streaming and memory (§9).** Music and ambience stream; effects decoded in memory stay within the 48 MB budget, with an LRU if needed; report how this is measured.
7. **Proving it without sound.** A debug audio trace (a setting) logs every play, skip, steal and crossfade with key, bus, volume and pitch, so the scripted runs and tests can assert behaviour.
8. **Tests.** Fallback chain, variation and pitch with a seeded random source, cooldown, voice cap and stealing order, distance skip, crossfade timing, bus volumes and mute, background pause and resume (as pure state).
