### Goal
A guild has shared storage: members deposit and withdraw items and currency as their rank allows, every move is atomic between the character and the storage (rule 10), and every move is in a log members with the right can read.

### Requirements
1. **Storage**: per guild, stored in the database, holding item entries (permanent ids, cards and refine kept) and currency; its size (entries) in content. Opened from the guild panel (decide whether a storage NPC is needed too, and why).
2. **Permissions**: new terms in the guild permission vocabulary (deposit, withdraw items, withdraw currency, read the log; decide whether withdrawals have a daily limit per rank, numbers in content). Checked by the server in the same transaction as the move, so a rank change racing a withdrawal cannot let one through.
3. **Moves**: deposit and withdraw run on the tick for the character and as one transaction for the storage, re-checking everything (the character still carries it, weight and entry limits, storage size, currency limits, permission). A part of a stack becomes a new entry with a new id. The character's side and the storage's side land together or not at all, also across a crash (a save that never lands) and on two server processes at once: two members withdrawing the last unit must not both get it. Worn items and items content marks not tradeable cannot be deposited.
4. **Log**: every move (who by character id, what by item and entry id, quantity, currency, when), kept for a time in content or configuration, readable by rank; nothing else about the person. Erasing an account (T-703) removes it from the log or leaves only ids (decide with STORE_COMPLIANCE.md, and update it and the erasure test).
5. **Disbanding** (T-904 deletes a guild's rows): decide what happens to what storage holds (for example: refused while it holds anything, or handed to the last leader, never silently deleted) and say why.
6. **Dupe safety**: a test per trick (the same entry twice, drop or use during a deposit, two withdrawals racing on two stores, a crash on either side, a disconnect, a rank change racing), plus property-style tests over random sequences counting every item id and coin across characters and storage.
7. **Client**: the storage window on a phone in landscape (contents, a quantity picker, currency, the log for those who may read it); all strings in `UiText`; the new states in the `ui` matrix with 0 problems on the smallest phone at the largest UI scale; say how you measured against ARCHITECTURE §9.
8. **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE (the guild section, §3 if saving changes, §10 messages), VOCABULARY, STORE_COMPLIANCE.md, ADR-002 rows from 480.
9. **Tests** for every rule with the real database where it matters; one e2e run: a member deposits an item and currency, another with the right withdraws it, one without the right is refused, the log shows both moves.
