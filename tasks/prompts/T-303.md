### Goal
Combat reads well on a phone screen. Every hit the server reports shows up at the right moment as a swing, a reaction, a damage number and a small effect, and a death looks like a death. All of this is presentation only: outcomes stay the server's (hard rule 1).

### Scope

### Requirements
1. **Timing (§7.6).** A `hits` entry plays the attacker's attack clip and, at its impact moment, the target's hit reaction and the damage number. The impact delay is theme data per rig or model, with a safe default. It's aligned with the render delay, so a hit never shows before the attacker reaches range. A late or dropped batch never replays old hits (row 101).
2. **Damage numbers.**
   - Pooled, world-anchored labels that rise and fade. Hit, crit and miss look different: crit is larger and has an accent, and a miss shows the word from `UiText`.
   - Damage to your own character looks different from damage you deal.
   - Numbers from many attackers on one target spread out instead of stacking.
   - Cap the numbers on screen; past the cap, drop the oldest or merge them, and say which you chose.
   - No allocation per hit after warm-up. Text stays readable at every UI scale (T-211).
3. **Hit effects.** Add `vfx.hit.normal`, `vfx.hit.crit` and `vfx.death.generic` keys to the manifest, generated as small placeholder scenes by `themegen`. Resolve them through the theme chain with fallback (§7.7). Effects are pooled, with a cap. Stay within the §9 draw-call and triangle budgets with 40 characters fighting.
4. **Death.** The die clip plays once and holds its last frame while `dead`. The corpse fades out when it leaves view. Your own death keeps the death screen (row 102).
5. **Target frame (row 139).** Add a change counter for entity HP in the view model, so the HUD's target frame shows an HP bar that updates only when `hp` changes. Never show exact HP for others, only the fraction the server sends.
6. **Level up.** A short effect and HUD flash on `level_up`.
7. **Budget and battery.** No per-frame work when nothing is animating; pooled everything; report draw calls, triangles and allocations in a fight with 40 characters.
8. **Tests.** Impact timing against the render clock, crit/miss/own classification, number spreading and the cap, no replay after catch-up, the HP change counter, effect fallback.
