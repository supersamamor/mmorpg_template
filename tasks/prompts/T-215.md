### Goal
T-214's load test put 200 bots in one view, and each client then received about 15 KB/s, over the 10 KB/s budget. In a crowded town or
boss fight the visible set must stay bounded, so cap it per observer, the way MMO clients do.

### Requirements
1. **Cap.** Add `aoi.max_visible` to `balance.yaml`, with a comment. Pick the value from the §9 budget and say how; about 60 is a
   starting point. An observer sees at most that many entities from its window.
2. **Who is always visible.** These are never cut, and count toward the cap:
   - the observer itself;
   - its current attack target;
   - any entity that is attacking it;
   - party members later (leave a documented hook, no party code).
3. **The rest.** Order it by Chebyshev distance, then entity id, so the result is deterministic.
4. **Hysteresis.** An entity near the cutoff must not flicker in and out every tick:
   - an entity already shown stays until it is beyond the cutoff rank by `aoi.cap_hysteresis`, a balance value;
   - or it leaves the window.
5. **Hits.** Entries in a `hits` batch are only sent for entities the observer currently sees, so this is consistent with T-307.
6. **Cost.**
   - No allocation per tick after warm-up.
   - Use a partial selection (nth-element or a bounded heap); don't sort everything.
   - Keep the T-204 fast path: an observer whose window didn't change costs a few reads.
   - Report the AOI phase cost at 100 and 200 observers, before and after the change.
7. **Measure.**
   - Re-run T-214's load test at 200 bots, all in one view, and report per-client KB/s. It must be ≤ 10 KB/s.
   - Re-run 50 bots and show no regression: the cap is not reached there.
8. **Tests.**
   - The cap holds.
   - The always-visible rules hold.
   - Nearest wins, with deterministic tie-breaks.
   - Hysteresis: no flicker for an entity sitting right at the cutoff for 100 ticks.
   - `hits` are filtered to visible entities.
   - A crowd test where 300 entities converge on one observer.
