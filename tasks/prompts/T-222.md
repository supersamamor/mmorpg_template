### Requirements
1. **Toon material, as theme data.**
   - A three-step cel shader (a ramp texture or steps in the shader) for characters, equipment, monsters, terrain and props. Glowing parts are unlit.
   - It works with `GearMesher`'s vertex-colour baking, so a dressed character stays one surface.
   - Shaders and ramps are referenced from the theme by asset key (hard rule 3). A theme without them falls back to today's look, with one warning.
2. **Outlines near the camera.**
   - An outline (inverted hull or the cheapest method that works on the Mobile renderer) on characters, monsters and interactable objects within a client-config distance, about 10 cells by default.
   - None on terrain, and none beyond that distance.
   - A dressed character costs at most 2 draw calls with its outline.
   - Measure the crowd peak with `tools/loadtest --spare-slots`. It must stay under 100 (row 164); if it can't, lower the default distance and say what you chose.
3. **Lighting and world.** A warm directional key light with one shadow, a flat ambient, a soft sky gradient and light distance haze, all as theme environment data. Shadows stay within §9: say the shadow map size and its cost.
4. **Placeholders.** Change `themegen` so that:
   - placeholder characters and monsters have the ADR-003 proportions (head about a third of the height, the face tipped toward the camera) and the job colours from ADR-003 point 4;
   - monsters use the shared palette.

   Keep the rigs, attach points and clip names unchanged, so animation and equipment keep working. `themegen --check` stays clean, and the regenerated pack is byte-stable.
5. **Readability.** Windowed screenshots at the default zoom and at the closest zoom: the own character, the three jobs if the scripted run can show them, a monster fight with damage numbers and statuses. Compare them with the style study, and point out any visible difference from option A.
6. **Tests.** Outline selection by distance and kind, the material fallback when a theme lacks the toon resources, the theme keys resolving, and the themegen proportions and colours.
