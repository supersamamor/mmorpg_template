### Goal
Players learn skills from their job's tree with skill points, change job when they meet the requirements, and cast skills: SP cost, cast time, cooldown, targeting and weapon requirements all come from data, and the server decides everything.
Passive skills add their modifiers. Monster and player casts show as cast bars. A player's summons are on its side.

### Requirements
1. **Learning.** `learn_skill` spends one skill point to raise a skill in the player's current job tree (and the parent jobs' trees) by one level, up to the tree's `max_level` (else the skill's), only when every `requires` entry is met. Every refusal has a reason term. Learned levels and points are kept per character in the progression store.
2. **Job change.** `change_job` moves the player to a job whose `parent_job_id` is its current job and whose `requirements` are met (base level, job level, quests, unlock). Decide and document what happens to job level, job EXP, unspent skill points and learned skills (keeping the parent's skills is the usual rule). Job stat bonuses and usable weapon types change with it. Raise events on the bus.
3. **Casting.** `use_skill` (skill, level up to the learned level, a target entity or a ground cell as the skill's `target` says) is checked by the server: learned, not `prevents_skills`, enough SP, off cooldown, weapon type allowed, range and line of sight above range 1, the right side for `friendly`, not hostile in a safe zone.
   - The player walks into range like an auto-attack, then casts for `cast_time_ms` (with the caster's `cast_time_mod` if the formulas have one) standing still; moving or a new order cancels it, and SP is spent when the cast ends.
   - The effects run through T-501's runner, with the same path as monster casts. The cooldown starts when the cast ends.
   - All numbers from data and formulas.
4. **Passives.** A learned `passive` skill applies its `passive_modifiers` with the source `skill:<id>`, updated when its level changes, and can't be cast.
5. **Allied summons (ADR-002 row 205).** A player's summon is on its owner's side: it follows and fights for its owner and doesn't attack its owner or the owner's side. Keep it simple and document the rule.
6. **Wire and client (§5.4, §10).**
   - Cast start and end for every caster in the AOI (player and monster, row 204), with the skill id and cast time.
   - The own skill list (learned levels, points, cooldowns left) in the own-player messages.
   - Never send SP costs, cooldowns or effect numbers beyond what the bundle already shows.
   - The client gets a skill bar with cooldown sweeps, a skill tree panel (learn, requirements greyed out), tap-to-target and ground targeting for touch, and a cast bar over any caster, plus a job change button when one is available.
   - Keep T-211's touch rules and the §9 budgets.
