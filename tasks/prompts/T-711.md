### Goal
Real art (T-707, a fork's) may be textured. Today a textured body, face, hairstyle, outfit or worn piece stays a surface of its own, because each `.glb` embeds its own copy of a texture: a character can cost several draw calls and a crowd breaks the §9 budget. Make a textured character one draw call again, and stop paying for job models that are only a fallback.

### Requirements
1. **Group by image, not by file.** Merge parts whose materials use the same image content (decide how to know it is the same: a hash of the pixels at load, the manifest naming an atlas key, or both; say why) into one surface. Parts with different images: decide between an atlas built at load (bounded size, mipmaps, what it costs in memory and time on a phone, done off the main thread) and a documented rule for artists (one atlas per theme, by asset key), and say why. Vertex colours (T-706's skin, hair and cloth) keep working on textured parts.
2. **The theme contract.** `docs/THEMES.md` says exactly what an artist does for one draw call per character, what costs a second one, and what `validate --theme` checks (add the checks to `ThemeCheck`: a character part whose texture would split the merge is a warning, with the asset keys).
3. **Job models only when needed.** Job models are preloaded although they are only drawn when a body is missing (row 314): load them only when that happens, without a hitch the player sees (measure).
4. **Placeholders.** The default theme stays untextured; for the tests and the measurement, generate a small textured test theme in the test or a temporary theme folder (never committed art in `themes/default`), with at least two parts sharing one image and one part with another.
5. **§9 budgets.** Measure before and after with T-706's crowd measurement (`--measure-looks`, headless where possible; at most one windowed measuring run, off screen, and check first that the window stays off screen: ADR-002 row 346). Report draw calls, triangles, memory and the worst frame for a crowd of 40 in textured looks and outfits.
6. **Tests:** grouping by image (same pixels in two files merge; different ones do not; a missing or broken texture falls back without a crash and warns once), the atlas or the rule, vertex colours kept, `ThemeCheck`'s new warnings, job models loaded on demand.
