### Goal
Currency can be earned but not spent (row 268). Add a shop: an NPC service where players buy and sell items for currency. Each buy or sell is **one inventory transaction**, items and currency together, all or nothing (hard rule 10).

### Requirements
1. **Shops as data.**
   - A shop definition (`shop.*` id) lists its stock: item ids, and optionally per-shop prices that override the item's buy price.
   - Validate it at load: every item exists and every price is positive.
   - Opened by `open_service` with a `shop` service and a shop id from the dialogue data. Decide how the shop id travels (a field on the action, or a generic service parameter) and document it.
2. **Buying.**
   - `shop_buy` (item, quantity) is refused unless:
     - a shop service is open from an NPC in talk range;
     - the item is in that shop's stock;
     - the quantity is within a data-driven limit;
     - the player can pay;
     - the bag can hold it (weight and slots).
   - Every refusal has a reason term.
3. **Selling.**
   - `shop_sell` (bag slot, quantity) pays the item's sell price.
   - Items with no sell price, and worn or quest-held items, are refused with reasons. Decide whether starter and quest items can be sold, and document it.
4. **Atomicity and safety.**
   - Prices come from the server's data only; the client never sends a price.
   - Guard against overflow on quantity × price.
   - Tests show a failed check changes nothing.
5. **Data.**
   - Add a merchant NPC in the starter meadow with original naming, using an existing `model.npc.*` key or the §7.7 fallback.
   - Give it a dialogue that opens the shop.
   - Stock: potions and the starter gear, plus the reset items Realign Tonic and Blank Page (row 251).
   - Price everything in `content/items/**` that the shop sells or buys. Keep the numbers sane against T-602's quest currency rewards, and say how many quests or minutes of selling drops buy a potion stack.
6. **Client.**
   - A shop panel with buy and sell tabs: the stock with prices; the player's bag with sell prices; a quantity picker; the currency balance.
   - Touch targets of at least 48 dp. It closes when the dialogue or service ends.
   - The client's own words go in `UiText`. Keep T-211, the §9 budgets and the T-222 look.
   - Add the panel to the `-Run ui` layout and touch-target check.
7. **Tests.**
   - Buying and selling, with every refusal;
   - atomicity (a failed bag check leaves the currency unchanged, and the reverse);
   - overflow;
   - load validation;
   - the wire messages;
   - a scripted client run step: talk to the merchant, open the shop, buy a potion, see the balance and the bag change, sell something, close.
