### Goal
The owner asked for a minimap (2026-11-28). This reverses ADR-002 row 428's choice, so it must answer that row's two reasons: no free corner of the phone HUD at the largest scale, and the cost of drawing the map a second time every frame.

### Requirements
1. **No second render of the world.** The minimap's picture of the map is made **once, at map export** (from the baked logic grid, or a top-down render the export step makes), shipped as a theme asset by asset key (rule 3) and listed in the asset manifest; at run time the minimap draws that one texture plus a few marks. No second camera or viewport rendering the 3D scene each frame. Say what it costs per frame and how you measured (ARCHITECTURE §9).
2. **What it shows**: the player (with facing), party members on this map (from `party_members`, already sent), warps and NPCs that the client already knows from the display bundle or what it sees, and monsters and other players **only while they are in the area of interest** (what the client already receives). Nothing the server does not already send: no spawn areas, no monsters out of view, no routes (rule 11). Decide whether quest targets are marked, from what the client already knows.
3. **Room on the HUD**: decide where it goes and at what size so the layout check shows 0 problems on every screen, notch and UI scale (the smallest phone at the largest scale included): for example a small corner map that can be collapsed to a button, and tapping it opens a full-screen map with the same marks. If it cannot fit in some layouts, say which and what shows instead. T-902's edge marks: keep them for party members off the minimap, or remove them, and say why.
4. **Input**: tapping the full map may walk there (a `move` intent the server checks as any other), or not: decide and say why. Pinch or zoom only if it fits the budget.
5. **Maps without a picture** (a fork's new map before export): the minimap hides itself rather than failing, and `export-maps.ps1 -Check` reports a missing or stale picture. `validate` checks the asset key is in the manifest.
6. **Options**: show or hide in the settings, kept on the device like the other preferences.
7. **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE (HUD and map export), MAP_AUTHORING.md (the picture is made by the export), THEMES.md if a fork must supply anything, VOCABULARY if a term is added, ADR-002 rows from 530, and row 428 marked as superseded by the owner's request.
8. **Tests**: the picture is made and checked by the export, marks are placed right for a known grid and camera, only known entities are marked, the layout matrix has the new states with 0 problems, the preference is kept. One e2e run: a scripted client with the minimap on walks, and the player mark moves; a second client in its party shows on it.
