### Goal
T-215 caps each observer's view at `aoi.max_visible` (50), ranked only by distance. In a crowd, nearby players push out monsters and NPCs: at 50 bots,
kills fell by about a third and deaths roughly doubled, and a quest NPC in a busy town could vanish. Make the cap fair by kind, without losing the bandwidth budget.

### Requirements
1. **Reserved slots, as data.** Add reservations by kind to `aoi` in `balance.yaml`, each with a comment:
   - a minimum share of the cap for monsters, NPCs and ground items. Choose the numbers and say how;
   - a slot a kind doesn't use goes back to the others, so a view is still filled nearest first.
2. **Ranking.** T-215's always-visible rules still come first. Then fill each kind's reservation with its nearest entities, then the remaining slots nearest first, with deterministic tie-breaks.
3. **The viewer's own loot.** A ground item this observer may pick up now ranks ahead of other ground items.
4. **Monsters that can reach you.** A hidden monster must never land the first hit: a monster chasing or aggressive toward this observer counts as always visible if the AOI can learn this from events. Use the event bus (rule 7); keep the T-215 protection against a long train bypassing the cap, and state the bound.
5. **Hysteresis.** T-215's no-flicker guarantee holds across kinds and across reservation changes.
6. **Cost.** No allocation per tick after warm-up, and keep the fast path. Report the AOI phase cost at 100 and 200 observers, before and after.
7. **Measure.** Re-run `tools/loadtest` at 50 and 200 bots: per-client KB/s (max ≤ 10), kills, deaths. Report before and after. Kills and deaths should move back toward the values without a cap. If they don't, say what you found rather than tuning until they do.
8. **Comment fix (row 121).** Correct the `aoi.*` comment: T-206 measured a view about 22 wide at the top edge and about 11 deep, split 7 ahead and 4 behind. No value change there.
9. **Tests.** Each reservation holds in a crowd; unused slots return; own loot ranks first; a chasing monster is visible before its first hit; a train stays bounded; no flicker over 100 ticks at a boundary; determinism.
