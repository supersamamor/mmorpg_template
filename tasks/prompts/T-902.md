### Goal
Players can form a party: invite, accept or decline, leave, kick, hand over the leader. Members see each other's HP and SP and where they are, share EXP from kills by a formula, share quest kill credit, and talk on a party channel through T-901's seam.

### Requirements
1. **The party** lives on the server, in memory, on one server process (say what happens to a party when a member's server process differs, or why it cannot in v1). Size limit and invite timeout in content or configuration (rule 2; decide which and why). One party per character. A member who disconnects stays a member through the grace period and leaves when it ends (decide; say why). The leader leaving hands the leader to the next member by a rule you state. A party of one ends.
2. **Invite** by character name or by tapping a nearby player: the invited player sees who asks and accepts or declines; a pending invite expires. Refusals have their own terms (full, already in a party, not online, blocked the inviter through T-901's blocks: answered like "not online", so a block does not show). Rate-limit invites per account.
3. **EXP share.** When a monster dies, the EXP goes to the party members who are on the same map instance and within a range (content) and alive, split by an expression in `content/formulas.yaml` (inputs whitelisted in VOCABULARY.md, for example the share count and level spread; decide whether the split is even or by level, and whether there is a level-gap rule, and put any numbers in content). The killer's EXP without a party must not change: golden tests prove it. Job EXP too, the same way or say why not.
4. **Quest kill credit** through the event bus (rule 7): a party member's kill counts for the `kill` objectives of members in range as content says. Quests do not call party, party does not call quests.
5. **Members' state**: each member gets the others' HP and SP (rounded or as fractions: decide what is enough and costs least), map and cell, at a rate you set (not every tick), and only for members, never for anybody else (the area-of-interest rules still hold for the world).
6. **Party chat**: register a `party` channel with `ChatService.Register` whose members are the party; mute, rate limit and reports work as on every channel; the client's channel switch gets it.
7. **Client**: a party panel (members with HP and SP bars, leader mark, leave, kick and hand over for the leader), the invite prompt, and members shown on the map: there is no minimap yet, so decide between a small minimap within the HUD budget and marks at the screen edge pointing to members off screen, and say why. All strings in `UiText`, the new states in the `ui` matrix with 0 problems on the smallest phone at the largest UI scale, within ARCHITECTURE §9; say how you measured.
8. **No client authority**: the client never says who is in range, who gets EXP or who killed what.
9. **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE (a party section, §10 messages), VOCABULARY (every new term, formula input and event), STORE_COMPLIANCE.md only if anything about a person is stored (it should not be), ADR-002 rows from 420.
10. **Tests** for every rule: invite flow and its refusals and expiry, leader hand-over, grace period, size limit, EXP split golden values (one, two and a full party, a member out of range, dead, on another map), solo EXP unchanged, quest credit through the bus, members' state only to members, party chat with mute. One e2e run: two scripted clients form a party, one kills a monster, both get EXP as the formula says, they talk on the party channel, one leaves.
